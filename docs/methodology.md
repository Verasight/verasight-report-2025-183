# Methodology

Methodology summary for 2026 Predictions Survey #2025-183.

## Survey

- Sponsor: Verasight
- Field dates: 2025-12-22 to 2026-01-05
- Population: United States adults
- Mode: online panel (Verasight Community)
- Questions: 89
- Question-level base sizes vary by question and are listed in `data/questions.csv`.

## Weighting

- Weight variable: weight for AI and predictions questions; weight_popculture for pop culture questions
- CPS variables: age, race/ethnicity, sex, income, education, region, metropolitan status
- Partisanship source: Pew Research Center NPORS benchmarking surveys, three-year running average
- Vote benchmarks: Population benchmarks of 2024 vote
